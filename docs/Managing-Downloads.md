# Managing Downloads

## The sidebar

The sidebar has two sections:

- **Downloading:** what's running, paused, stopped or failed. Its header shows how many are downloading and the total
  speed. Click **+** next to it to open a `.torrent` file.
- **Downloaded:** what has finished, with each one's size and when it finished. It appears once something finishes.

Active rows show a progress bar. A finished download drops into **Downloaded**; a failed one shakes.

Each row has a button at its end:

- **Pause** or **Resume** while it runs;
- **Retry** for a failed link download;
- **Show in Finder** once it's done on this Mac.

## Search

Type in **Search Downloads** at the top of the sidebar. Both sections show only the downloads whose names match.

## The toolbar

With a download selected, the toolbar has:

- **Show in Finder** and **Open** (Open is for a single file). Both are off until the file is on disk.
- the download's own actions: **Pause** or **Resume**, **Retry**, **Priority** and **Cancel**, depending on what it is
  and its state;
- **Move download to Trash**, the red button.

When the window is narrow, the tools show only their icons, then fold into a **…** menu.

## The row's menu

Control-click a download in the sidebar:

- **Open With:** open a single file in its default app or another app, or show it in Finder.
- **Pause** or **Resume**, and **Retry** for a failed link download.
- **Remove from List** (finished downloads): takes it off the list. The files stay in Downloads.
- **Move to Trash:** takes it off the list and moves its files to the Trash. For a download on another device, the
  file stays on that device.

## What Get keeps when you quit

- **Running downloads continue.** A torrent or link download that was running when you quit continues at the next
  launch, from where it was. Get doesn't ask first, since it continues your own download.
- **Paused stays paused.** A download you paused comes back paused, and nothing transfers.
- **Your choices stay.** Paused and removed files and file priorities come back as they were.
- **Finished downloads stay** in **Downloaded**, and Open With and Show in Finder still work.
- **Cancelled and trashed downloads don't come back.**
- **Downloads on another device** that hadn't finished aren't listed again.

To keep a download from continuing at the next launch, pause it, cancel it or move it to the Trash before you quit.
