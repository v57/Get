# Settings

<!-- platforms: macos -->

Choose **Get ▸ Settings…** (**⌘,**). Settings has one pane, **Privacy**.

## Privacy

**Use iCloud** turns iCloud sync on or off. Get uses iCloud for one thing: the list of your devices that
**Download on** offers (see [Download on Another Device](Download-on-Another-Device.md)). The vault's records are
encrypted on this Mac before they're synced.

It's one switch for every v57 app on this Mac: turning it off here turns it off in the others too. Turning it off asks
first, with **Turn Off iCloud?**. Every v57 app on this Mac then stops syncing through iCloud and iCloud Keychain.
What's already in iCloud stays, and your other devices keep it. Turn the switch back on to sync again.

## Updates

Update checks aren't in Settings: they're in the **Get** menu. **Check for Updates…** checks now, and
**Check for Updates Automatically** checks once a day. It's off when you install Get. See
[Privacy and Security](Privacy-and-Security.md#update-checks).

## On Linux and Windows

The Linux and Windows builds have no Settings window yet, and iCloud isn't available there.
